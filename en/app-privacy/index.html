Azangoo Android App – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

This policy applies to the Azangoo Android app. The Azangoo website has its own policy.

Your privacy matters. This is what Azangoo does with your data.

What stays on your phone

Your place and your settings (adhan, voice, language, theme) are saved only on this phone, and map pictures you have seen may be kept there for faster loading. Azangoo has no account, no ads and no analytics, and nothing about you is sent to a server of ours (voices, duas and lunar event texts are only downloaded from it, see below). If you turned on Android’s own backup, your settings may be part of that backup.

Location

It is used only if you allow it, and only while the app is open (Azangoo never reads your location in the background), to find your place and calculate the times, and it is saved on the phone as your default place. You can choose a city yourself and use the app without ever allowing location.

Services we contact

To find a city name, the time zone and the weather, the app sends only coordinates (your place, or a spot you tap on the map) or the text you search for to these free services: OpenStreetMap Nominatim and Photon, Open-Meteo, MET Norway and timeapi.io. Map images come from OpenStreetMap and Esri, and Street View opens Google Maps. Like any website, these services can see your IP address, and they have their own privacy policies. Adhan voices, duas and lunar event texts are downloaded from our own server (azangoo.com) and kept on your phone; the app only asks for the lists and the files and sends nothing about you, but like any website the server can see your IP address.

Permissions

Location: to find your place. Notifications: to show the adhan and reminders. Exact alarms: to start the adhan on time. Battery optimization: the app only opens Android’s settings page, so you can stop the system from halting the adhan. Vibration: for the vibrating alert of the adhan. Start at boot: to set the adhan alarms again after the phone restarts. Foreground service and keeping the phone awake: so the adhan keeps playing when the screen is off. Internet: for the services above and for downloading voices, duas and lunar event texts.

Compass

The Qibla compass uses your phone’s motion sensors. The readings are used only on the screen; they are not saved or sent.

Crash report

If the app crashes, the error text is saved on the phone and shown to you the next time, so you can decide whether to send it. It is never sent automatically.

Children

Azangoo does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Clear the app’s data or uninstall it, and everything is removed from the phone.
